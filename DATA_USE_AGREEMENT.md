# Data Use Agreement

**Dataset:** A paired dataset of voiced and silent Korean commands with headset facial and head-motion signals, Zenodo, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23853036

**Data provider:** The research team at Kangwon National University, Republic of Korea, represented by Woojoo Kim, Division of Liberal Studies (woojoo.kim@kangwon.ac.kr) ("the Provider")

**Recipient:** The researcher named in Section 10 and the team members listed there ("the Recipient")

The dataset contains facial movement weights, head-motion data and voiced audio recordings of 20 participants aged 18 to 35 years who consented to the use of their data by other researchers for research purposes. Voice recordings and movement data can identify a person even without a name. This agreement sets the conditions under which the Provider makes the dataset available.

## 1. Access
1.1 Every person who will access the dataset, including students and researchers without an institutional affiliation, provides their name, position or role, affiliation if any and contact email address, and signs this agreement. Students do not need a supervisor's co-signature.

1.2 The Provider verifies each person's identity through an email address at their institution or other verifiable information, such as a professional profile corroborated by publication records. The Provider uses these details only to verify identity and keeps a record of who has received the dataset.

1.3 Each person requests access through the Zenodo record and emails the signed agreement to woojoo.kim@kangwon.ac.kr. The blank agreement is publicly available at https://github.com/kimhaejun9568/korean-voiced-silent-commands/blob/v1.0/DATA_USE_AGREEMENT.md.

1.4 The Provider grants access to every person who meets Sections 1.1 and 1.2, without assessing the intended research, institution type or nationality. The Provider responds to requests within 10 working days.

## 2. Permitted use
2.1 The Recipient will use the dataset only for research and analysis carried out by the people named in Section 10. Research in academic and commercial settings is permitted.

2.2 The Recipient will not sell or license the dataset or any part of it.

## 3. Ethics approval
The Recipient is responsible for obtaining any ethics approval or exemption that their institution or local regulations require for the use of human data.

## 4. Protection of participants
4.1 The Recipient will not attempt to identify any participant, for example by voice, by linking the data with other information, or by any other means.

4.2 The Recipient will not attempt to contact any participant.

4.3 If the Recipient becomes aware that a participant could be or has been identified, or that anyone not named in Section 10 has obtained the dataset, the Recipient will stop using the affected data, will not disclose any identity, and will inform the Provider within 10 working days.

## 5. No redistribution
5.1 The Recipient will not share, publish or otherwise transfer the dataset, any part of it, or copies of the recordings to anyone who is not named in Section 10. Other users must obtain the dataset from the Provider themselves. New team members may access the dataset only after completing Section 1 and being added to Section 10.

5.2 The Recipient will not publish audio recordings or audio excerpts in any form, including as supplementary material or demonstrations.

5.3 Publications may report results, statistics and figures derived from the data, provided they do not allow any participant to be identified.

5.4 Models or other outputs derived from the dataset may be shared only if they cannot be used to reproduce the audio recordings or to identify participants.

## 6. Data security
6.1 The Recipient will store the dataset on secure, access-controlled systems and will not upload it to public or unsecured services, including public generative-AI tools.

6.2 The Recipient will delete all copies of the dataset when they no longer need it for research or when this agreement is terminated.

## 7. Withdrawal of consent
If a participant withdraws consent, the Provider may ask the Recipient to delete that participant's data. The Recipient will do so within 30 days and confirm the deletion in writing.

## 8. Citation
Publications using the dataset will cite the dataset DOI and the Data Descriptor that describes the dataset.

## 9. General terms
9.1 The dataset is provided as is, without any warranty. The Provider is not liable for any loss arising from its use.

9.2 The Provider may terminate this agreement if the Recipient breaches it. On termination the Recipient will delete all copies of the dataset and confirm the deletion in writing.

9.3 This agreement remains in force as long as the Recipient holds any copy of the dataset.
